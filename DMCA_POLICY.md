# Copyright / DMCA Policy — Toonect

Last updated: 2026-08-25

Toonect connects comic writers with manga, manhwa, and manhua artists.
Writers post story briefs and artists showcase portfolio work — all of it
user-uploaded content. Toonect respects intellectual property rights and
responds to valid copyright complaints.

## Reporting infringing content

If you believe content on Toonect infringes your copyright, send a notice
to **Budoessien2331@outlook.com** including:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
2. Identification of the specific material on Toonect you claim is
   infringing (a direct link/URL to the profile, brief, or portfolio
   piece), with enough detail for us to locate it.
3. Your contact information: name, address, phone number, email.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized
   by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the
   notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized
   to act on the owner's behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## What happens next

On receiving a valid notice, we will remove or disable access to the
reported material and notify the user who posted it, including the
substance of the claim. The affected user may submit a counter-notice if
they believe the material was removed in error, identifying the material
and stating under penalty of perjury that they have a good-faith belief it
was removed by mistake or misidentification.

## Repeat infringers

Accounts with repeated, confirmed copyright violations will have their
content removed and their accounts terminated.

## Ownership of your own uploads

Posting a story brief or portfolio piece to Toonect does not transfer
ownership. You keep the rights to your own work. By posting, you grant
Toonect a license to host, display, and distribute it as needed to operate
the platform (e.g. showing it to other users, in search/discovery). You
confirm, by uploading, that you own the rights to what you post or have
permission to post it.

## Designated agent

To get formal DMCA safe-harbor protection under US law, Toonect's
designated agent should also be registered with the US Copyright Office's
DMCA directory (https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/, $6 filing fee)
once the platform has real users — an internal policy alone isn't enough
for the legal safe harbor, only for good practice ahead of that.
