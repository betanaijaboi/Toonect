# Privacy Policy — Toonect

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## What we collect

- Account info: email, display name, password (handled via Supabase Auth —
  we never see or store your raw password).
- Profile content: portfolio pieces, story briefs, availability status.
- Messages sent through the platform's real-time messaging.
- Uploaded files (art, portfolio images) stored via Supabase Storage.

## Why we collect it

To run the core service: creating your profile, matching writers with
artists, enabling messaging, and displaying your portfolio/briefs to other
users.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** — our database, auth, storage, and real-time messaging
  provider. See [Supabase's privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any
time. Deleting your account removes your profile and uploaded content;
messages sent to other users may remain in their conversation history.

## Data retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active, or until you request
deletion.

## Security

Authentication and storage are handled through Supabase's managed
infrastructure (encryption in transit and at rest). We don't independently
store your password.
